# My modelling rules

_Added at the end of lessons, in my own words, with a date._

- **2026-10-04: Check the model against the hardware.** As many screws on the
  part, as many holes in the model, in the same places. (Found that the holes
  were only on one side although there are screws left and right.)
- **2026-10-04: Added thickness? Check the screw length.** A new wall between
  the head and the thread eats the thread; either the screw is longer by
  exactly that thickness, or a counterbore for the head.
- **2026-10-04: The assignment is the intent; the instructor decides.** If the
  instructor allowed it differently, I do it differently, but I know the pros
  and cons.
